07 / FAIR QUESTIONS

Before we
connect.

Less mystery. More clarity.

What happens to my data and account access?+

We agree on which information the workflow needs and use the narrowest practical permissions. Prototypes start with sample data. Before connecting your accounts, we document which providers process your data, what is retained, and how you can revoke access. This website’s demos run locally; the brief form opens your email app.

What if the AI gets something wrong?+

AI can make mistakes. I design checks and explicit escalation paths, and keep client-facing messages in drafts for review. We test known failure cases together. A flagged transaction is a prompt to investigate, not a definitive finding.

Are AI and platform running costs included?+

Provider usage and automation hosting can create recurring costs. We estimate them separately from the build, agree on who pays, and discuss limits before launch. The time-value calculator above does not deduct those costs.

Who owns the workflow?+

The project agreement will specify ownership and handover. The intended setup is your accounts, an exportable workflow, and documentation you can use. Third-party software and services retain their own licenses and terms.

How long will a build take?+

It depends on the integrations, data quality, and review requirements. After discovery, I’ll propose milestones and a delivery estimate for the agreed scope. We start with one useful process rather than promise a timeline before seeing the work.
